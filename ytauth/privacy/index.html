ProfGames

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

What ProfGames is

ProfGames is streaming software that runs on the streamer's own computer. It connects to the streamer's YouTube channel so the stream can show channel numbers and live chat.

What it asks Google for

One permission: read-only access to your YouTube account (youtube.readonly). It cannot upload, post, edit, delete or change anything on your channel.

What it reads

  • Your channel's subscriber, view and video counts.
  • Your live broadcast status and live chat messages while you stream.

Where your data goes

  • Everything stays on your computer. It is used to show on your own stream.
  • Your Google sign-in token is saved in a file on your computer and is never sent to us.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your YouTube data, and do not use it for advertising.

Removing access

Remove ProfGames at any time from your Google Account permissions. Deleting the ProfGames folder on your computer removes the saved token and anything it logged.

Google API Services

ProfGames' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
